Why MBA Scholarships Matter

An MBA is a significant investment in your future. With rising education costs, scholarships provide much-needed financial relief. They not only reduce your financial burden but also recognize your academic potential and achievements.

Types of MBA Scholarships Available

  • Merit-based scholarships: Awarded based on academic performance
  • Need-based scholarships: For students requiring financial assistance
  • Category-based scholarships: For specific groups like women, SC/ST candidates
  • Corporate scholarships: Sponsored by companies for employees

How to Apply for MBA Scholarship at Amity Online

  1. Fill out the online application form
  2. Submit required documents (academic transcripts, ID proof)
  3. Complete the eligibility assessment
  4. Receive scholarship confirmation

Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for the MBA scholarship at Amity Online University, you need:

  • A bachelor's degree from a recognized university
  • Minimum 50% marks in graduation (relaxation for reserved categories)
  • Valid entrance test scores (if applicable)
