Amity Online MBA Eligibility Criteria

Amity offers a flexible eligibility structure, making the Online MBA accessible to learners from diverse academic backgrounds.

Eligibility for Indian Students

  • Minimum 40–50% marks (varies by category)
  • Basic English proficiency

Eligibility for Foreign Students

  • Graduation equivalent to Indian standards
  • Certificate of equivalence from Association of Indian Universities (AIU)

What's the Cost? Amity University Online MBA Fees Explained
